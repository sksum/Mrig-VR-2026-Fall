# Story seeds — one is picked at random for each chapter

1. **The Boulder and the Vow** — A knight discovers the tallest object on the desk and declares it a sacred mountain. The other must be convinced to help climb (walk around) it.

2. **The Case of the Missing Goblet** — An object that was on the desk last chapter has vanished. The knights investigate, accuse each other, and eventually hold a funeral for the lost relic.

3. **The Border Dispute** — The two knights draw a border across the desk and argue over territory. Negotiations (dialogue) fail; a duel at the center follows. Loser kneels, winner cheers.

4. **The Sentry's Night** — Steel Knight stands guard while Copper Knight tries to sneak past to steal something on the far side of the desk. Sneak, block, chase.

5. **The Newcomer** — A new obstacle has appeared on the desk (check `query_obstacles` against what you remember). The knights debate whether it is friend or foe, send Copper to inspect it, and react to what they "find".

6. **The Potion Merchant** — Copper Knight sells "potions" (drink animation) harvested from near an obstacle. Steel Knight is a skeptical customer. Haggling, a taste, a dramatic reaction.

7. **The Long Patrol** — A quiet chapter: the knights patrol the perimeter of the play area in shifts, trading weary observations about the state of the realm (the desk).

8. **Freeform** — Look at the actual obstacle layout from `query_obstacles` and improvise a chapter that uses every detected object as a prop.
